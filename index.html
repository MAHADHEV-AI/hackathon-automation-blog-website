FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We've Got Clear Answers.

Everything you need to know about using HackScale to dominate your next competitive hackathon.

What is HackScale and how does it help me win hackathons? +

HackScale is an all-in-one hackathon operating system and knowledge platform. It provides high-performance project boilerplates, tested sponsor SDK integration snippets, 48-hour velocity trackers, automated pitch deck creators, and field-tested playbooks so you spend 90% of your sprint on core innovation rather than boilerplate and configuration chaos.

Can I use HackScale starter templates for commercial or student hackathons? +

Yes! All our boilerplates and field code snippets are released under permissive MIT licenses. They comply with all major hackathon guidelines (such as Major League Hacking, ETHGlobal, and Devpost rules), which allow starting from open-source boilerplate frameworks as long as the core project code is authored during the event.

How does the Sponsor Bounty Fast-Track work? +

Hackathons often feature $5,000 to $20,000 in sponsor bounties (e.g., "Best Use of Gemini", "Best Polygon Integration"). HackScale analyzes the exact rubric requirements and gives you drop-in TypeScript wrappers, authentication configs, and verification scripts to ensure your submission meets every single criteria required by sponsor judges.

What is the 3-Minute Demo Rule mentioned in your field notes? +

Most hackathon judges evaluate 30 to 50 projects in under 3 hours. They give you roughly 180 seconds. Our rule specifies allocating exactly: 30 seconds to the visceral human problem, 90 seconds to a live working demo (showing the 'magic moment'), 30 seconds to the technical architecture, and 30 seconds to business viability. Our pitch deck studio builds your slides around this exact formula.

Is HackScale free to read and use for our upcoming sprint? +

Yes, our entire Field Notes library, judge rubric simulator, and core boilerplates are completely free. If your team wants automated war room sync, Figma pitch deck exports, and priority cloud deployments, you can upgrade to the Sprint Pro tier.
