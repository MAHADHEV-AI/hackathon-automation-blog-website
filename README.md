## 🌾 About MGH Farm

MGH Farm is a 42-acre family-run livestock and poultry farm operating since 1997 in Salem. We raise healthy livestock, pasture-grazed goats, heritage country chickens (*Naatu Kozhi*), and supply farm-fresh meat, organic eggs, and day-old chicks directly from our farm gates.

### What We Offer:
- **Country Chicken (Naatu Kozhi):** 100% free-range native breed chickens fed on natural grains, butchered fresh to order into curry or biryani cuts.
- **Fresh Broiler Chicken:** Antibiotic-free, tender chicken cuts and boneless breast fillets.
- **Salem Farm Goat Mutton:** Pasture-grazed goat meat, curry cuts, and succulent ribs/chops.
- **Organic Country Eggs (Naatu Kozhi Muttai):** Golden-yolked, wholesome daily collected eggs.
- **Farm Fresh Poultry Eggs:** Sanitized and fresh daily breakfast eggs.
- **Starter Chicks:** Healthy, vaccinated day-old chicks for home and commercial poultry farmers.
