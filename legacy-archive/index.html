02Our story

Welcome to MGH Farm

Quality livestock, trusted farming.

At MGH Farm, we are passionate about raising healthy livestock and poultry and providing quality farm products to our customers across Salem and Tamil Nadu.

Our farm focuses on chickens, goats, eggs, and chicks, with attention to proper care, healthy growth, and responsible farming practices. We believe that good food starts with caring for our animals and maintaining a clean, chemical-free farm environment.

How we started

From a passion for farming to a growing livestock farm.

MGH Farm started with a passion for farming and livestock. With dedication and hard work, we began raising chickens and goats and gradually expanded our farm to provide a wider range of quality farm products.

Today, MGH Farm serves customers looking for healthy chickens, goats, fresh eggs, and chicks for their homes, farms, and poultry needs.

What we offer

Healthy animals and quality farm products.

We care for every part of our farm so customers can choose products with confidence.

  • Chickens Heritage Country Chickens (Naatu Kozhi) and healthy birds raised with proper care and attention for different customer needs.
  • Goats Quality goats raised with proper natural grazing, clean water, and a healthy farm environment.
  • Fresh eggs Nutrient-dense country and farm eggs collected fresh every morning, handled with supreme cleanliness.
  • Chicks Healthy, active flock starters for customers who want to start or expand poultry farming.

Our commitment

Healthy animals are the foundation of a successful farm.

At MGH Farm, we give importance to healthy livestock and poultry, proper feeding and care, clean and hygienic surroundings, quality farm products, customer satisfaction, and responsible farming practices.

Growing with you

Farming is a journey that requires patience, dedication, and care. At MGH Farm, we are proud to be part of that journey and look forward to growing together with our customers.

Thank you for being part of our journey.

05Kitchen notes

Salem Style Cooking

Authentic Farm Recipes.

How to cook traditional South Indian Country Chicken (Naatu Kozhi) and Salem Mutton Chukka to tender perfection.

Village Style

Salem Naatu Kozhi Kulambu

Real country chicken needs gentle, slow braising with stone-ground shallots, dry red chillies, and freshly roasted coriander. Pressure cook for 4–5 whistles with fresh sesame oil for deeply rich, aromatic bone gravy.

⏱ Prep: 15m | Cook: 40m Best cut: Naatu Kozhi Curry Cut
Special Occasion

Attur Mutton Chukka Varuval

Tender mutton cuts pan-roasted in cold-pressed coconut oil with whole peppercorns, curry leaves, and ginger-garlic paste until dark and crisp on the edges while remaining juicy inside.

⏱ Prep: 10m | Cook: 35m Best cut: Fresh Goat Mutton Chops & Ribs
